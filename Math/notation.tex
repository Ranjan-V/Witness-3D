All claims concern finite supplied inputs; there are no experimental results.

\paragraph{Conventions.}
Camera axes are right/down/forward. A camera-to-world pose has rotation $R_{wc}$ and center $C$; its inverse is $R=R_{wc}^{\mathsf T}$, $t=-RC$. Pixels are undistorted, on the original returned depth grid. Reference depth is never part of a repair or detection input.
\paragraph{Outcome semantics.}
VERIFIED FEASIBLE means an exactly checked point exists for the encoded system. VERIFIED CONTRADICTION means no point exists in its declared domain, established by an exact checker. UNKNOWN includes all unverified numerical results. Physical attribution requires the separate trust assumptions.
\paragraph{Uncertainty.}
Deterministic containment is an assumption on every true inlier measurement. A statistical interval is not a deterministic bound. $s_{ki}$ is not assumed calibrated. Cameras represented by finite floats are interpreted as exact rational input numbers for algebraic assembly; physical calibration uncertainty is separate.
